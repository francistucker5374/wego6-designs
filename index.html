buy-wego6.com · design direction · 27 July 2026

Pick one

Three directions, same German content, same conversion structure. All three are QA-clean: zero console errors, zero horizontal overflow at 375/768/1440, no remote images, every CTA already wired to /de/go/. Whichever you pick gets fanned out to all 50 pages.

One change I'm making to whichever you choose. All three came back using "Test" / "Testbericht" / "Testurteil". In Germany that wording is only allowed if the page documents genuine first-hand testing (UWG §5b(3)) — and German results for this exact product are already being patrolled by consumer bodies. It becomes "Einschätzung" / "Redaktionelle Bewertung", which reads the same to a buyer and carries none of the exposure.
Direction A

Editorial Verdict

Consumer-testing magazine. Prüfbericht layout, scored sub-criteria, olive & burnt orange.

Strongest authority signal. Reads like an independent testing institute — masthead, issue number, scored breakdown for Rezeptur / Anwendung / Preis-Leistung.
Highest perceived credibility of the three
Biggest legal exposure. "Prüfbericht Nr. 06/2026", "Redaktionelle Produktprüfung" and the three sub-scores all assert a formal test we haven't run — and the look sits close to Stiftung Warentest's trade dress. Stripping those claims removes most of what makes it distinctive.

Direction B · my pick

Clinical Apothecary

European pharmacy language. Blister-strip hero drawn in SVG, Apotheke green, mono numerals.

The most distinctive and the most defensible. A six-pocket blister strip authored entirely in SVG — foil gradients, embossed lot text, perforation — carries the "6" in the name. The verdict is a calibrated measurement scale with a needle rather than stars, and pricing is framed as pharmacy pack sizes N1/N2/N3.
Trust language that fits a supplement without claiming to be a lab
Every number set in mono — dosage, 387 mg, prices all read as specification
The verdict device relabels cleanly to "Einschätzung" with zero design loss
Drops the ® after WEGO6 — we haven't verified a registered mark

Direction C

Warm Momentum

Premium DTC wellness. Sunrise gradient system, ingredient-coloured cards, amber CTAs.

Most commercial. Warm sunrise palette pulled from the formula itself — sea buckthorn, amber, cayenne — with each of the seven ingredients carrying its own botanical colour. A "metabolism current" gradient runs behind the hero and through the three benefit axes.
Likely the best raw conversion of the three — feels expensive, not shouty
Warm palette differentiates hard from the clinical white competitors already ranking
Reads more like a brand's own site than an independent review — slightly weaker fit for the "seriös / is it legit" queries that are our biggest traffic cluster
